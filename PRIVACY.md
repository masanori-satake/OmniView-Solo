# Privacy Policy - OmniView-Solo

OmniView-Solo is designed with user privacy as the top priority.

## 1. Data Collection and Use
OmniView-Solo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or usage data to external servers. All operations are performed locally on your device.

## 2. Camera Access
This extension requires camera access to capture and process whiteboard images.
- **Local Processing**: All captured video frames are processed entirely within the browser's memory.
- **No Transmission**: No video or image data is ever uploaded to any server.

## 3. Local Storage
Settings such as camera roles and custom labels are stored locally in your browser using `chrome.storage.local`. This data is used only to improve your user experience and is not accessible by the developer or any third parties.

## 4. Disclosure to Third Parties
Since no data is collected, we do not provide, sell, or share any information with third parties.

## 5. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the GitHub repository.
